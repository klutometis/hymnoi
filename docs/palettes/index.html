Four candidates for site/palette.css, measured: CIEDE2000 between kinds under normal vision and three simulated dichromacies, WCAG contrast of labels on the paper #faf6ec, and a round trip through the ISO Coated v2 (FOGRA39) CMYK profile. Generated by build.py.

Recommendation: pigmenta

Adopt pigmenta: cp docs/palettes/pigmenta.css site/palette.css. It is the only candidate that clears every target and still belongs on cream paper beside EB Garamond: seven pigments a Greek painter could have ground (cinnabar, verdigris, yellow ochre, Egyptian blue, murex purple, carbon black, green earth), set so that the gods' cinnabar is the most saturated colour on the sheet (C 64) and the places' green earth the quietest (ΔE 22 from the paper, 2.0:1), so a field of 112 small place dots recedes and the 67 gods come forward. Its closest pairs are 27.4 in normal vision (nymph/place) and 14.0, 14.4, 14.4 under deuteranopia (nymph/group), protanopia (mortal/group) and tritanopia (nymph/mortal): no two kinds collide, so nothing has to be carried by shape. Every fill survives a FOGRA39 round trip within ΔE 0.55. Okabe–Ito, adapted here, measures a little better under simulation (≥ 15.8) but its orange, pink and sky blue read as a chart rather than a book, and it needs six text variants to pigmenta's four. Three fills are too light for Greek labels on the paper (verdigris 4.2:1, ochre 2.9:1, green earth 2.0:1); the CSS comments give text colours of the same hue (and a deeper cinnabar for gods, which keeps the god and hero labels apart for deutans) that reach 4.6:1 or more and stay ≥ 21.7 apart, ≥ 10.8 under each simulation. The node labels in the graph are ink, so these matter only in the reading panel, and using them needs a --<kind>-text hook in style.css.

Method

  • Colour difference. sRGB (D65) to CIELAB, CIEDE2000 with kL = kC = kH = 1; the implementation reproduces Sharma, Wu & Dalal (2005) test pairs to four decimals. Targets: ≥ 20 between any two of the seven kinds in normal vision, ≥ 10 under each simulation.
  • Colour-vision deficiency. Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) matrices at severity 1.0 (full dichromacy), applied in linear RGB. The swatch strips and the little graphs above are drawn in the simulated colours.
  • Text. WCAG 2 contrast against #faf6ec; 4.5:1 is the bar for the Greek labels. Where a fill falls short, or where darkening would make two labels collide, the palette names a text colour of the same hue (±12°, at least ¾ of its chroma) in its CSS comment. The second block of four columns in the summary measures the seven colours actually used for text. The baseline file names none, so its text colours are the fills darkened along constant hue until they reach 4.6:1.
  • Print. littleCMS through Pillow's ImageCms: sRGB → ISO Coated v2 (basICColor), which is the FOGRA39 characterisation for coated stock → sRGB, relative colorimetric, no black-point compensation; ΔE₀₀ between the colour and its round trip. A second trip through PSO Coated NPscreen (FOGRA43) is shown beside it. A round trip under 1 means the colour is inside the press gamut; every fill of the three new palettes is (worst 1.00, Tol's pale cyan, at the edge). CMYK values are the FOGRA39 separations, for the printer. The paper colour itself would be a light tint on white stock, or the stock.
  • Attention. "God chroma rank" is where the god colour falls when the seven are ordered by CIELAB chroma; "place salience" ranks the seven by ΔE₀₀ from the paper, and "quietest" means places are nearest the paper of all kinds, yet every place colour stays ≥ 22 from it so a 3 px dot is still seen. The graphs use the real 286 entities, sized by mention count as the site does (×0.62), with nine hymn discs for scale.
  • How the new palettes were made. Differential evolution (SciPy) over CIELCh for all seven colours, maximising the worst of (normal ΔE / 20, each simulated ΔE / 10) with penalties for leaving the FOGRA39 gamut, for any kind out-chroma-ing the gods, and for any kind sitting nearer the paper than the places. Pigmenta is confined to hue windows for its pigments (cinnabar 28–42°, verdigris 155–190°, yellow ochre 72–86°, Egyptian blue 250–285°, murex 325–355°, green earth 100–135°, carbon black C ≤ 6); Tol and Okabe–Ito are tethered to their published colours (≤ 4.9 and ≤ 8.1 ΔE₀₀).
  • Caveats. CIEDE2000 is calibrated on patches of a few degrees; a 3 px dot subtends far less, and small fields lose chroma first (blue–yellow worst), so real dot-to-dot differences are smaller than the table. Greek labels, not colour alone, identify each node on the graph, and the legend repeats the colours. Machado at severity 1.0 is the worst case; most colour-deficient readers are anomalous trichromats and see more than the simulations show.
