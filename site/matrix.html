Ὕμνοι the names, hymn by hymn

graph matrix

The thirty-four Homeric Hymns across, every being and place they name down, grouped by kind. A mark is sized by how often the hymn names it, by name, epithet, patronymic or periphrasis; a ring marks the god a hymn is addressed to.

Hover a mark for the lines; click a name or a mark for every citation, and a hymn’s numeral for the hymn in the graph. Columns by length gives each hymn the width of its lines and puts a tick at each line that names the being: the four long hymns, to Demeter, Apollo, Hermes and Aphrodite, hold four lines in five.

Greek: Evelyn-White (1914), via Perseus; Hymn 34, Εἰς Ξένους, from Allen (1912). A draft: see REVIEW.md for the judgement calls still open. Printing this page gives a 24 × 38.8 in poster.
