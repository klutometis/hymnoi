Ὕμνοι the gods, mortals and places named in the Homeric Hymns

Every name in the thirty-four Homeric Hymns, read line by line in the Greek. The numbered discs are the hymns, sized by length; every other node is a god, nymph, hero, mortal, group, creature or place, sized by how often it is named. A line joins a hymn to each name in it.

Hover to trace a node’s links; click one to read the lines that name it. Gods as hubs folds the hymns into their gods, as Peter Danenberg’s hand-drawn graph of 2009 did.

Greek: Evelyn-White (1914), via Perseus; Hymn 34, Εἰς Ξένους, from Allen (1912). A draft: see REVIEW.md for the judgement calls still open.
