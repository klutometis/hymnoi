Ὕμνοι the places of the Homeric Hymns, and who gathers round them

graph · matrix · map

Every place the Hymns name, pinned where it lies (identifications from the Pleiades gazetteer; lands at a central point, in italics). Nothing else is pinned: each hymn is drawn toward the places it names, and each god, hero or mortal toward the hymns that name them.

Places no one can locate (Nysa, Ortygia, Erebos) float with the rest, in hollow circles. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan; labels appear as you zoom. Click anything for its lines.
